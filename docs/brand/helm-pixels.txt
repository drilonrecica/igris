# The helm in pixels (V17-18)

Reference art for the TUI's helm (SPEC §15.4, internal/look/helm.go), drawn
by hand from the mark (igris-mark-dark.svg) and the icon (igris-icon.svg).
One character is one pixel; a terminal cell holds two pixels stacked, drawn
with `▀` (top in the foreground color, bottom in the background color), so
each grid is half as many rows tall in cells. Approved by the owner
2026-10-23: L on large screens, S-tile on small ones.

Legend (truecolor):

    .  transparent (the terminal's own background)
    O  outline, glow        #3CC8FF
    n  face, left half      #0A1124
    m  face, right half     #05070F
    r  ridges               #236C92
    V  visor, glow          #3CC8FF
    C  visor core           #B8F0FF
    b  chin bars            #32A3D3
    R  plume                #E3172B
    p  plume's inner stroke #F05A6E
